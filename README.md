# Spending Habits of Young Adults in Nigeria

## Overview

This project analyzes the spending habits, income levels, savings behavior, and financial discipline of young adults in Nigeria.

It was carried out as part of **Week 7 of the MIHAMS Graduate Trainee Program (Data Analytics Track)** using survey data collected via Google Forms and analyzed using Excel and Power BI.

---

## Dataset

- Source: Google Form Survey  
- Responses: 62 participants  
- Topic: Spending Habits of Young Adults in Nigeria  

---

## Tools Used

- Microsoft Excel  
- Power BI

## Key Insights

- Majority of respondents are aged **25–30**
- Most respondents earn between **₦50,000–₦100,000 monthly**
- Food, transportation, and bills are the major spending categories
- Spending is primarily influenced by basic needs rather than lifestyle wants
- Savings behaviour improves with higher income levels
- Many respondents remain uncertain about their financial health
